About Future Skills Fellowship

A life changing learning journey.



Six Weeks That Changed My Perspective

The Future Skills Fellowship was far more than a learning program, it was a transformative journey. Throughout six inspiring weeks, we developed practical skills, improved our confidence, collaborated with talented fellows, and learned directly from experienced mentors.

Every session encouraged creativity, leadership, communication, teamwork, and personal development. Each activity motivated us to think bigger and become better versions of ourselves.

The fellowship has left a lasting impact on my personal and professional growth, and I will always be grateful for this opportunity.


My Fellowship Journey

Being selected for the Future Skills Fellowship was one of the proudest moments of my academic journey.

I honestly never expected to be selected. More than 200 applicants reached the interview stage, but only 50 fellows were finally selected. I felt incredibly honored and grateful to be one of them.

Throughout this fellowship, I gained valuable knowledge, improved my communication skills, learned modern digital tools, built confidence, and formed meaningful connections with mentors and fellow participants.


Special Thanks

My heartfelt appreciation goes to Sir Aakash, the Founder of Leaders for Global Society (LGS) and the visionary behind the Future Skills Fellowship.

Thank you for creating an initiative that empowers young minds, nurtures leadership, and provides students with opportunities to grow beyond the classroom.

Your dedication, encouragement, and belief in students have inspired countless learners, including me. This fellowship has not only enhanced our skills but also strengthened our confidence to pursue greater goals in life.

Thank you to the entire LGS and FSF team for making this incredible journey unforgettable.
